⚠️ The Healthcare Crisis in Small Clinics & Emergencies

  • ❌
    Paper & WhatsApp Chaos: Over 70% of clinics in India manage appointments on manual paper diaries or fragmented WhatsApp messages, leading to 2-hour waiting room arguments and lost histories.
  • ❌
    Blind Ambulance Referrals: Emergency ambulances are dispatched without patient vitals or triage scoring, resulting in critical transit delays (>45 mins in metro congestion).
  • ❌
    Blood Shortage Disasters: Families frantically hunt for compatible blood units across hospitals during traumatic hemorrhages due to zero real-time inventory visibility.
  • ❌
    Preventable Medication Errors: Attending physicians lack automated allergy shields, leading to fatal Penicillin or NSAID drug cross-reactions.

✅ The PulseSync Software Solution

  • ✔️
    Unified Multi-Persona Hub: Distinct, tailored cockpits for Patients, Doctors, and Clinic Staff with dedicated permissions, vitals preview, and waiting token feeds.
  • ✔️
    Sub-Second C++ Geodesic Dispatch: Native Haversine routing (<5ms) that matches caller coordinates with the nearest available mobile ICU and computes traffic-calibrated ETAs.
  • ✔️
    Real-Time Blood Compatibility Matcher: Native ABO/Rh cross-matching algorithm with automated universal donor (O-) allocation and shortage broadcast.
  • ✔️
    Longitudinal Health & Allergy Shield: SQLite-backed records with neon clinical warnings, digital passes, and doctor home-visit distance tracking.
